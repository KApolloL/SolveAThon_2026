The piles

Authority decides the pile. Inside each pile, the writer ranks by an impact score whose weights they choose. Counts are for NC DHHS in the tool's opening scenario: a mid-level writer with about a month of runway who needs supervisor approval for match.

What we found

Of {{OPEN_STATE}} open opportunities that list state governments as eligible, {{NIH}} are NIH research grants. Only a handful of posted opportunities are genuine DHHS business; most of the real pipeline is forecasts. Match percentages appear in only 6 of {{TOTAL}} rows. They live in the full announcement, which a local AI model reads and quotes.

How we checked it

  • The team labeled 60 opportunities without seeing the AI's answers. The AI agrees on stated facts (match, construction, designation) 93–94% of the time, and on judgment calls much less often (67% on whether a state agency could lead the program).
  • The AI never assigns a tier. Written rules do, and every card shows the evidence.
  • We say what we didn't do: Tiers 1–3 were not hand spot-checked.
