# Team context (one page every reviewer and persona reads)

Last updated: Fri Nov 6, 2026, late. Draft filled from the repo after the problem release. Kent must approve it before a teammate runs a reviewer. Plain words. No personal data. Mark anything unconfirmed `[assumed]`.

- **Problem in one sentence:** NC agencies miss or find late federal funding opportunities because monitoring and matching to agency priorities is manual and uneven; OSBM wants an AI-assisted Federal Funds Matchmaker that surfaces promising leads early while humans make the final call. [from `docs/QUEST_BRIEF.md`; user detail still partly [assumed]]
- **Sponsor:** NC OSBM (Office of State Budget and Management). OSBM could fund a pilot.
- **Primary user (role only, no names):** an NC state agency grants lead or the person who inherited grant screening. Many agencies have no dedicated grant staff, so the tool must work for "the average person who does not normally do grants." They are time-poor, not grant experts, and work against close dates. [from `planning/07_discovery_findings.md`]
- **Supervisor / decision maker:** the user's division director or agency leadership, who decides whether staff time goes to a pursuit; and OSBM, which decides whether to fund a pilot. [assumed from the discovery notes]
- **Decision and action:** the user decides which open or forecast opportunities are worth pursuing for their agency, early enough to act. The result should let them open the top matches, check eligibility and fit from the stated reason, and tell their supervisor "pursue" or "pass" before the close date. A human decides; the tool never applies, scores or ranks as final. [from `planning/02_framing_worksheet.md`]
- **What we are building:** one scored table of every opportunity against every agency. The agency picker is the main screen; a second tab groups by opportunity. Clearly ineligible items are hidden by default with a visible count and a toggle. Every match shows a deadline and time-to-act, a plain reason with a plan quote and a notice quote, and a score breakdown. Rules decide the flag; the score only orders. It must run on Azure. The scoring engine is deterministic; an LLM only labels text with quoted evidence and never decides. [from `planning/08_product_decisions.md`]
- **Current state (Fri Nov 6 night):** opportunities are ingested (6,523 rows: 882 posted plus 2 API-only, 588 forecasted, 4,947 closed since 2024-11-06, 104 Federal Register notices). All 34 OSBM plans have text; 158 goal rows. **Not built yet:** `data/manual/criteria.csv` is missing, `src/docs/fact_sheet.md` is empty, the real ranking has not run, and the Azure app has no public URL (Azure OpenAI not created). Reviewers must separate what is built and verified from what is planned. [from `handoff.md` and repo files; the counts are repo status, not slide facts]
- **Where outputs are (when built):** `data/processed/ranking.csv`, `data/processed/payload.json`, `outs/criteria_tool.html`, `outs/top_results.csv`, `outs/figures/`, `deliverables/slide_brief.md`. Most final outputs do not exist yet.
- **Product criteria (5, measurable):**
  1. **Eligibility:** `applicant_types`, `applicant_types_named`, `eligibility_text`. State government or unrestricted passes; otherwise flag with the reason. Blank means "check eligibility", not drop. Filter or flag.
  2. **Deadline:** `status`, `close_date`, `is_forecast`. Days left = close date minus `REF_DATE` (2026-11-06). Closing-soon threshold lives in `src/R/00_config.R`. Filter and score.
  3. **Fit:** `title`, `summary`, `cfda_list`, and agency plan goals in `src/docs/agency_goal_summaries.csv`. Embedding plus keyword match; LLM labels only with a quote copied from the source. Score.
  4. **Payoff:** `award_ceiling`, else `est_total_funding / n_awards`. Log scale. Missing is unknown, never zero. Score.
  5. **Burden:** `cost_sharing`, `funding_instrument`. Match or cost-share and applicant burden are a penalty. Score.
  - Tie-breaker, not a criterion: `nc_won_before` (USAspending name heuristic; 348 recipients still unclassified).
- **Success test:** three pool agencies (Department of Public Schools, Department of Environmental Quality, Department of Health and Human Services), 20 labelled items each, two labelers per item, labels relevant / maybe / not relevant, 70% recall in the top N. A known-bad item must rank last or be filtered with a reason. The Clean School Bus case (CFDA 66.045 [uncertain]) must appear for education and environment. The same input must give the same output on rerun. Labels are "team-labelled", never ground truth. [from `planning/02_framing_worksheet.md` and `08_product_decisions.md`; not yet run]
- **Judging criteria (released):** Understand (real users shaped the build), Frame (problem and requirements), Solve (works; effective use of cloud services), Evaluate (measured own output, honest failures, defended tradeoffs), Drive Impact (could win a pilot budget from OSBM).
- **Named threats from the organizers:** false positives, false negatives (recall versus manual monitoring), uneven agency documentation, inconsistent data quality.
- **Known weaknesses to name:** recall versus shortlist length; stale or thin agency plans (Auditor, Elections, Wildlife Resources, Commerce); missing sources (SAM.gov, USDOT page, private foundations, federal budget and earmarks); OCR text in DHHS; 70 far-future close dates (some 2099); 104 notice rows with no deadline; eligibility codes not yet verified; the flag threshold may be tuned on the same pool used to report recall; an LLM can invent a quote unless the quote check runs.
- **Data used:** Grants.gov extract and API, Simpler.Grants.gov (mirror and cross-check), Federal Register notices, USAspending (NC winners, name heuristic), 34 NC agency strategic plans from OSBM. Public data only. No personal or participant data.
- **The talk:** 6 minutes with a brief demo plus 2-minute Q&A, in person. Judges and NC government staff, mostly non-technical. Cover user and problem, solution, evidence including where it fails, pilot, and scale roadmap. Lead with the answer; five parts: ask, why, how, limits, next step.
- **Evidence rules:** every number on a slide or in a handout must be in `src/docs/fact_sheet.md` with the script that reproduces it. If it is not there, treat it as unverified. A simulated persona is not user evidence. Log every AI use in `AI_USAGE_LOG.md`.
