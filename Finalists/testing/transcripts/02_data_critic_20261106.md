## 2. Three most damaging weaknesses
1. Nothing is built or frozen, so the evidence is plan-only; tradeoff: freeze a smaller proven pipeline (keyword fit + table + recall on hand labels) rather than promise embeddings, tiers and feedback.
2. Recall on a 20-item, keyword-biased, open-only pool does not support the "70% recall" claim; tradeoff: report intervals and by stratum, accept that the claim is "indicative", or label more items.
3. Date, award and eligibility gaps (117 no deadline, 197 stale forecast dates, 723 no award, 635 without a state code) can hide or demote the very items the user wants; tradeoff: show unknowns visibly and lose a clean score.
