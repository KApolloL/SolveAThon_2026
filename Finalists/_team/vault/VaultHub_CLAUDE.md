## Solve-a-thon judging rubric
Source: Claude memory `solveathon-judging-rubric`, rubric given by Kent on 2026-09-29. The 5-minute video to agency leadership and the slides are scored on five criteria.
- Understanding: are the match criteria specific to this agency and this user, or generic? Did they actually drive the workflow?
- Framing: were the problem and requirements appropriately framed?
- Solving: is the method sensible and reproducible, and would an agency want to apply it? No bonus points for complexity that does not improve the matches.
- Evaluating: were the methods and output evaluated thoroughly? Teams that name a real weakness and their tradeoff score higher than teams that claim perfection.
- Driving Impact: can a non-technical budget director watch the presentation and know what to do next?
How to apply:
- Map every slide to one criterion.
- Name weaknesses openly (for example: Tiers 1-3 were not spot-checked; the AI is weaker on judgment calls).
- End with concrete decisions for a budget director.
- The slide brief is `src/docs/presentation_brief.md` in the SolveAThon_2026 project (location not verified).
