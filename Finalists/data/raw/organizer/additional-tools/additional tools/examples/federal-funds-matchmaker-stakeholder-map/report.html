1. Summary

The solution's success depends most on OSBM leadership (sponsor), OSBM analysts, and agency grant managers (daily users). These groups must be managed closely through co-design and regular demos. Agency leadership makes the final decision to apply and NCDIT security & privacy must approve any pilot, but both are currently only moderately engaged; raising their interest early is the biggest stakeholder lever. Agency program staff and grant-writing consultants may be sceptical that AI could replace their judgement or work, so position the tool as decision support with a human always in the loop.
