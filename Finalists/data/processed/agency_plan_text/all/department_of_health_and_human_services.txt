INTRODUCTION
The North Carolina Department of Health and Human Services is
operating in a complex and rapidly evolving environment. Resource
limitations, changing requirements and ongoing system pressures
require the Department to remain ﬂexible, responsive and focused on
delivering essential services. These conditions affect how programs
are designed, implemented and sustained, and they underscore the
importance of thoughtful planning and clear priorities to best serve
North Carolinians.

At the same time, this moment highlights the vital role of public service.
North Carolinians rely on NCDHHS and its partners to ensure access to
care and maintain systems that promote health, safety and stability across
communities. This strategic plan provides a framework for advancing
that responsibility by strengthening access to services, supporting
whole person well-being for all North Carolinians and transforming and
modernizing health care delivery while investing in a sustainable and
skilled workforce and promoting organizational excellence.

PRIORITY AREAS
④ PRIORITY 1: Deliver Critical Services to Improve the Health and Well-being
              of North Carolinians
Support timely and coordinated implementation of NC Medicaid, the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance
Program (SNAP) and child welfare and child care services, with a focus on maintaining access, minimizing
disruption for beneﬁciaries, and supporting state and county DSS partners.


④ PRIORITY 2: Transform Rural Health
Strengthen and sustain programs, including the Rural Health Transformation Program, that enable rural
communities to thrive, leveraging full federal investments and prioritizing stakeholder engagement.


④ PRIORITY 3: Strengthen Behavioral Health Crisis Care and Its Coordination with
              the Justice System
Improve North Carolina’s behavioral health crisis system to better support individuals with mental health
needs, including those who are justice-involved.

④ PRIORITY 4: Partner with Key Stakeholders to Drive Reforms that Promote
              Affordable and Accessible Health Care for Every North Carolinian
Advance health care affordability and access by leveraging the Department’s expertise, engaging
partners, and proposing innovative solutions that lower costs and expand access to high-quality services.

MISSION
In collaboration with our partners, DHHS provides essential services to improve the health, safety
and well-being of all North Carolinians.




VISION
Advancing innovative solutions that foster independence, improve health and promote well-
being for all North Carolinians.




VALUES
            Accountability
            We hold ourselves responsible for the work we do and outcomes achieved to display
            credibility and maintain public trust.

            Communication
            We maintain an open and trusting environment by listening to gain a full understanding
            of issues and sharing our message thoughtfully to reach a variety of audiences.

            Creativity
            We proactively identify and imagine ways to advance our mission, being inspired to
            seek opportunities and take action.

            People First
            We are driven by our commitment to serve others, building lasting relationships with
            stakeholders and continuously elevating the service we provide.

            Respect
            We interact with others in a manner that demonstrates our esteem for them and
            acknowledges the value of their thoughts, opinions and ideas.

            Teamwork and Collaboration
            We recognize that we are one department, one team, working toward one goal: to
            improve the health, safety and well-being of all North Carolinians.

            Transparency
            We share expertise and information readily within our divisions, department and with
            our stakeholders and the community.

    GOAL 1: ACCESS TO CARE
    Support North Carolinians’ ability to safely, easily and fully
    access the services, programs and systems of care of NCDHHS
    and our partners.



    OBJECTIVE 1.1: Maintain access to health and human services in
    traditional and non-traditional settings to meet North Carolinians
    where they are.



    OBJECTIVE 1.2: Mitigate the social, cultural and literacy barriers that
    prevent North Carolinians from accessing health and human services.



    OBJECTIVE 1.3: Support affordable care options that North Carolinians
    can access easily.



    OBJECTIVE 1.4: Promote policies and remove barriers that improve North
    Carolinians’ ability to access health and human services.

GOAL 2: WHOLE PERSON WELL-BEING
Improve the well-being of North Carolinians by delivering
comprehensive, high-quality services that address complex
factors shaping outcomes for individuals and families.



OBJECTIVE 2.1: Coordinate and integrate health and human services
systems to support a healthy North Carolina.



OBJECTIVE 2.2: Simplify the processes for families and individuals
to navigate mental health, substance use disorder, intellectual/
developmental disability (I/DD) and traumatic brain injury (TBI)
systems and services.



OBJECTIVE 2.3: Improve systems that address non-medical drivers of
health such as access to healthy food.



OBJECTIVE 2.4: Strengthen service delivery across all of NCDHHS
points of contact by improving quality, efficiency, and reliability.

    GOAL 3: TRANSFORM AND MODERNIZE HEALTH
            CARE IN NORTH CAROLINA
    Transform North Carolina’s health care ecosystem by
    modernizing delivery, strengthening community and rural
    care, and optimizing strategic partnerships to expand access,
    improve outcomes and manage costs.



    OBJECTIVE 3.1: Align policy, ﬁnance and operations to manage costs
    and improve care.



    OBJECTIVE 3.2: Leverage volume purchasing power and develop
    strategic partnerships to increase access and reduce costs.



    OBJECTIVE 3.3: Leverage the expertise of state-operated health care
    facilities to support individuals with complex needs.



    OBJECTIVE 3.4: Promote a sustainable rural health delivery system
    that improves health outcomes for all North Carolinians.

GOAL 4: SUSTAINABLE WORKFORCE
Develop a skilled workforce that supports North Carolinians’
health and well-being through training, career pathways and
innovative strategies to attract and retain talent.




OBJECTIVE 4.1: Develop and strengthen career pathways that address
critical service gaps in health and human services workforces.



OBJECTIVE 4.2: Provide all workforce members supporting the health
and well-being of North Carolinians the necessary training, skills and
compensation to deliver high-quality care and services.



OBJECTIVE 4.3: Support individuals with employment barriers to
obtain work, build ﬁnancial security, increase independence, and
strengthen social capital.



OBJECTIVE 4.4: Leverage ﬁnancial incentives, data and innovative
practices to recruit and retain individuals to health and human
services workforces.



OBJECTIVE 4.5: Operate required workforce programs to efficiently
and effectively meet the needs of eligible North Carolinians.

    GOAL 5: ORGANIZATIONAL EXCELLENCE
    Promote efficient and effective operations through
    continuous improvement, leveraging data, modernizing
    systems, using resources wisely and strengthening
    accountability and transparency across the department.



    OBJECTIVE 5.1: Modernize technology systems to enhance efficiency
    and collaboration.



    OBJECTIVE 5.2: Foster a culture of data-driven decision-making
    across NCDHHS.



    OBJECTIVE 5.3: Strengthen governance systems that promote
    accountability, manage risks and align activities with NCDHHS goals.



    OBJECTIVE 5.4: Steward state resources and continuously improve
    organizational processes and practices.
