    Best match
    How many words a grant's title and summary share with your agency's plan goals. It is a keyword score, not a judgment of quality. Compare scores only inside one agency's list.
    Your agency can apply
    The listing's list of who can apply, or a quoted line in the announcement, says state agencies are allowed.
    Eligibility unclear
    The applicant list is unclear. The announcement's own words are shown under the title; read the announcement (the link on the title) before spending time.
    Who signs
    Who has to approve an application: you can start now, your boss must sign, or leadership must sign. Shown only when the announcement makes it clear.
    Closing soon
    14 days or fewer left to apply.
    Coming up
    Forecasts: the funder says it plans to post this. You cannot apply yet, but you can plan.
    Rolling
    No close date is listed. Check the announcement for how long it stays open.
    Pursue, Maybe, Pass
    Your own marks, saved only in this browser. Pursue puts the grant in your cart and opens a plan. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    Tier 1, 2, 3
    Who has to sign, as in the qualifier tool. Tier 1: you can start. Tier 2: your boss signs (matching money). Tier 3: leadership signs (matching money on an award of $1M or more).
    Odds (≈)
    A rough read of competition from the listing: how many awards will be made, who else is allowed to apply, and whether NC has won this program before. Good, Fair, Long shot, or Unknown when the listing says nothing. It never changes the ranking.
    Effort (≈)
    Easy, Medium or Hard from who signs and whether matching money is needed. The weeks shown (2, 4, 8) are our rough guess, not a measurement.
    Payoff (≈)
    The most one award can be, divided by those rough weeks of work. Shown only when the listing says your agency can apply. Use it to compare grants, not as a promise of money.
    Refine (optional)

    The list works with this closed. Everything here changes what is shown or how your own view is ordered; the stored keyword fit scores are never changed.
