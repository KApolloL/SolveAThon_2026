How these results were ranked:
Our tool sorted grant opportunities into three tiers, instead of a singular list. These tiers are differentiated by the level of authority needed from a grant coordinator's perspective. Tier 1 grants can be directly acted upon by the grant writer using the tool. Tier 2 opportunities would need approval by a supervisor. Tier 3 grants, due to high matching costs or other factors, need approval from the secretary or the legislature. The 3 tiers allow a user to make decisions about opportunities based on their capabilities and their priorities. Furthermore, within the tiers, the grants are sorted based on an "impact score" which is calculated with multiple metrics, including plan fit with the agency's strategic plan, people reached, help per person, award size, and program priorities. While the weights for these metrics were equal, a user of our tool can adjust these weights according to their priorities. Also, there are a few opportunities chosen for the DMVA on this list, to showcase the generalizability of our model.
